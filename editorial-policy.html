Editorial Policy

Last updated: August 2026

InvestingNoobs creates educational content for beginners learning about cryptocurrency, investing, trading, stocks, commodities and prop firms. We explain concepts clearly and responsibly, without promising profits.

Research and sources

We use official protocol documentation, regulator publications, public company filings, provider terms and recognised educational sources where possible. Market prices, laws and product conditions change, so readers should verify current details with the original source.

Writing and updates

We separate facts, examples and opinions; explain limitations and risks; and update articles when material information or links change. Course summaries are starting points, not substitutes for reading the full guide.

Risk disclaimer

InvestingNoobs is not a financial adviser. Crypto, stocks, commodities, derivatives and prop-firm evaluations involve risk, including the loss of capital. Nothing here is personal financial advice or a guarantee of performance.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links may be affiliate links. When a commercial relationship exists, we will identify it clearly. Commercial relationships do not determine our risk warnings or educational conclusions.

Corrections

Readers can report errors or outdated information through the website contact method. Credible corrections are reviewed and published when appropriate.
