Turn market noise into a repeatable process

01

Understand the asset

Separate a coin, stock, commodity, fund, derivative and wallet from the story built around its price.

02

Read the trade-off

Build your rules

Course FAQ

Questions beginners ask before they start

Is this crypto course free?

Yes. Every lesson and the final assessment are available in the browser without a download or paid subscription.

Do I need trading experience?

No. Start with lesson 1 and move in order. The course explains the vocabulary first, then introduces markets, analysis, execution and risk.

Does completing it make me a profitable trader?

No course can promise that. Investing and trading involve uncertainty and possible loss. This course teaches a safer decision process, not guaranteed returns or personal financial advice.
