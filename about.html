About us

InvestingNoobs is an independent project created to offer, for free and with easy access, a clear, real-time view of three markets: cryptocurrencies, commodities, and stocks of major companies.

Our goal

It's built especially for people who don't know much about these markets yet. You don't need to be an expert or have any prior experience: the idea is to lend a hand to anyone who wants to start understanding how these things work, without complications or spending money.

That's why everything we offer — the price tracker and the educational blog — is and will always be completely free. We gather data from public sources and explain it simply, without assuming any prior knowledge, so anyone can take a look and learn at their own pace.

Data sources

Cryptocurrency prices come from the public CoinGecko API. Metal prices come from Gold-API. Stock prices come from Stooq. All are publicly available market data providers.

What we're not

We are not financial advisors or a regulated entity qualified to give investment recommendations. The information we show is purely informational. Any financial decision you make is your own and your own responsibility — we recommend verifying the information and, if needed, consulting a qualified professional.
