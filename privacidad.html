Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit InvestingNoobs and how it is used, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018.

Who is responsible

Owner: Gonzalo Lequerica
Contact: gonzalo.lequerica@gmail.com

Browsing without an account

You can use the market tracker, the 50-lesson course and every tool without registering. In that case we do not ask for your name, your email or any personal data. Your watchlist (the ☆ list) and your course progress are then stored only in your own browser, never on our servers.

If you create an account for price alerts

Creating an account is optional and only needed for price alerts and for keeping your watchlist the same across devices. In that case we store:

  • Your email address and your password (the password is encrypted by the authentication provider; we cannot see it).
  • The alerts you create: the asset, the price level and whether the alert has already fired.
  • Your watchlist.
  • If you turn on notifications, the technical address your browser gives us to deliver them. It does not identify you or let us do anything other than send you a notification.

Legal basis: performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). How long: until you delete your account. You can delete your alerts at any time from the alerts panel, and ask us to delete the account by writing to the email above.

If you subscribe to the weekly email

We store your email address, the date you subscribed, where on the site you subscribed and an irreversible hash of your IP address (used only to stop automated sign-up abuse; it cannot be turned back into your IP).

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR). How long: until you unsubscribe. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also ask at the email above. We never sell, rent or share the list, and we do not send third-party advertising through it.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data, we do not build advertising profiles from it, and we do not send investment advice or personal recommendations about what to buy or sell.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. You can learn more and manage your personalized ad preferences at:

  • How Google uses information (policies.google.com)
  • Google Ads Settings

Automatic technical data

As with any website, the hosting server may automatically log basic technical data (such as IP address or browser type) for security purposes and aggregate usage statistics, without identifying specific users.

Market data sources

The prices shown are obtained from public third-party APIs (CoinGecko, Gold-API, Stooq). These requests are made directly from your browser to those services to display real-time data.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, accounts and notifications, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • GitHub Pages — serves the website files and receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google AdSense — advertising, as described above.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data by writing to gonzalo.lequerica@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how the site works. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
